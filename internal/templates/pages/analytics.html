Understanding Breakeven

The breakeven sales level represents the target revenue needed to exactly cover all your expenses. Any sales beyond this amount generate net income.

Equation:
Breakeven Point = Total Fixed Costs / Gross Margin %

Use this to play with scenarios. If you hire a new employee, how much extra revenue do you need to cover their salary? If you take out a loan, how does interest impact your daily targets?
